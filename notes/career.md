# Notes — career positioning (read 2026-09-23, index)

## Seniority expectations (AI-engineering interview rubric)
https://github.com/adilshamim8/ai-engineering/blob/HEAD/interview/03-get-hired.md
- Senior (5–8y): design the system, lead technical direction, own in production, hard trade-offs (model selection, safety vs capability, timeline vs quality).
- Staff+ (8+y): define strategy/roadmap, influence org (standards, hiring bar), navigate ambiguity, multi-system thinking.

## Role distinctions (SWE vs ML vs AI engineer)
https://medium.com/ai-architecture-and-engineering/why-software-engineers-have-an-unfair-advantage-in-ai-engineering-ba209dbf9398 (Aug 2026)
- ML Engineers ship models (training loops, distributed training, optimization math); AI Engineers ship products around models (evals, RAG, orchestration); SWEs build systems. "Software engineering experience is foundational, not merely adjacent."
