# Notes — courses, books, blogs (read 2026-09-23, index)

- Nathan Lambert, RLHF Book (2025–, living) — https://rlhfbook.com — open textbook, print via Manning, Discord community. Author leads Tülu/Open Instruct.
- DeepLearning.AI, "Fine-tuning & RL for LLMs: Intro to Post-training" (announced Oct 2025, Sharon Zhou) — https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/fine-tuning-and-reinforcement-learning-for-llms-intro-to-post-training — 5 modules: lifecycle, SFT/RLHF/PPO/GRPO/LoRA, evals + reward hacking + red teaming, data + synthetic data, production go/no-go + log flywheel.
- DeepLearning.AI RLHF course (2023, Nikita Namjoshi, older) — https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/reinforcement-learning-from-human-feedback
- Lilian Weng, "Reward Hacking in Reinforcement Learning" (Nov 2024) — https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2024-11-28-reward-hacking/ — oracle/human/proxy rewards; U-Sophistry (Wen et al. 2024, arXiv:2409.12822); sycophancy (Sharma et al. 2023).
- James Le's notes on Lambert's 13-lecture RLHF course (2026) — https://jameskle.com/writes/rlhf-course — "character training is preference optimization with the objective written down."
- Chip Huyen RLHF deep dive (May 2023, canonical older) — https://huyenchip.com/2023/05/02/rlhf.html; book "AI Engineering" (2025).
- RLHF/RLAIF/RLVF/RLEF taxonomy (Sep 2026) — https://github.com/trishambp/trishambp.github.io/blob/HEAD/_posts/2026-09-10-reinforcement-learning-for-llms-rlhf-rlaif-rlvf-rlef-swirl-post-training.md
- Community RLHF/DPO/GRPO/PPO tutorial w/ derivations (2026) — https://github.com/wanshuiyin/aris-in-ai-offer/blob/HEAD/docs/tutorials/rlhf_dpo_grpo_ppo_tutorial_en.md — secondary source; formulas match canonical.
- Preference-optimization survey (preprint, lit. through Sep 2026) — https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202506.2381 — includes single-T4 GPT-2 PPO case study; proxy-vs-judge mismatch caution.
